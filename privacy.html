Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This notice covers this website, applitrust.com. Any Applitrust service will have its own privacy notice when it is released.

What this site collects

This site has no sign-up forms, no accounts and no comment boxes, and it does not ask you for personal information. It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not load fonts, scripts or images from other websites.

Our website host may keep standard technical records, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and running. We do not use those records to identify you.

If you email us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it to reply to you. If you wrote to ask about private testing or to be notified about Applitrust, we may also contact you about that. We do not sell your email address and we do not share it with anyone for their marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep your email only as long as we need it for the reason you wrote to us. You can ask us to delete it at any time by writing to support@applitrust.com.

Who else sees it

Only the companies that provide our email and website services, and only so they can provide those services, or anyone the law requires us to tell.

Children

This site is not directed to children.

Changes

We may update this notice. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
