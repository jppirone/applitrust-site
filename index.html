What we check

  • Identity

    That the person is who they say they are.

  • Employment history

    We contact all of the candidate's prior employers* to verify work history, including title and dates employed.

  • Education

    Schools attended and degrees earned.

  • Certifications and licenses

    Credentials the person says they hold.

* Within reason. Some employment may not be verifiable.
